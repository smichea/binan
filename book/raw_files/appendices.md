## Heads of the Town / Mga Naging Pinuno ng Bayan

Under Spain the town was headed by a *gobernadorcillo*, often called *capitán*. Only a few names are known so far: Francisco Mercado, son of Domingo Lam-co, in 1783; members of the Alonso, Mercado and Alberto families in the decades that followed; Eduardo Gana, who built the Isabel bridge in 1850, according to the town’s history of 1953; and in June 1898 the revolutionary *presidente* Pedro de León, called Palañac. A large panel in the city museum, the *Listahan ng mga naging Pinuno ng Bayan ng Biñan*, seems to give the full list; it has yet to be transcribed. From 1901 the head of the town was the *presidente municipal*, from 1937 the mayor, and since 2010 the city mayor. The list below follows the portraits of the mayors in the museum, checked against other records ([Category:Mayors of Biñan | Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Mayors_of_Bi%C3%B1an)) ([Messages of the President, Book 2: José P. Laurel, vol. 2 | Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/MOP-Vol2-Jose-P-Laurel)) ([Messages of the President, Book 4: Sergio Osmeña | Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/MOPVol1and2SergioOsmena)) ([Messages of the President, Book 5: Manuel Roxas, vol. 2 | Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/AppointmentsAndDesignations1946-1948ManuelRoxas)) ([Rosenstock’s Manila City Directory, 1921 | Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/manila-city-directory-1921)) ([BCHATO accords pillars of good governance | City of Biñan, 2018](https://www.binan.gov.ph/bchato-accords-pillars-good-governance/)). The dates on the portraits were painted long afterwards and sometimes overlap, and two gaps remain.

Noong panahon ng Espanya, pinamumunuan ang bayan ng isang *gobernadorcillo*, na kadalasang tinatawag na *kapitan*. Iilang pangalan pa lamang ang kilala: si Francisco Mercado, anak ni Domingo Lam-co, noong 1783; mga kasapi ng mga pamilyang Alonso, Mercado at Alberto sa mga sumunod na dekada; si Eduardo Gana, na nagpagawa ng tulay ng Isabel noong 1850, ayon sa kasaysayan ng bayan noong 1953; at noong Hunyo 1898, ang rebolusyonaryong *presidente* na si Pedro de León, na tinatawag na Palañac. Mukhang ibinibigay ng isang malaking panel sa museo ng lungsod, ang *Listahan ng mga naging Pinuno ng Bayan ng Biñan*, ang buong listahan; hindi pa ito naisasalin sa sulat. Mula 1901, *presidente municipal* ang pinuno ng bayan, mula 1937 ang alkalde o punong-bayan, at mula 2010 ang alkalde ng lungsod. Sinusunod ng listahan sa ibaba ang mga larawan ng mga alkalde sa museo, na inihambing sa iba pang mga talaan. Ipininta nang matagal na panahon pagkaraan ang mga petsa sa mga larawan at kung minsan ay nagsasanib ang mga ito, at may dalawang puwang pa sa listahan.
