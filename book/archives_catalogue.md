# Biñan historical image catalogue

Compiled 2026-11-14 for the bilingual Biñan local-history book.
Every file listed in Part A was downloaded into this folder. Each one is public domain or under a free licence, and each one was checked by eye to make sure it actually shows Biñan. Biñan appears under the old spellings "Viñan" and "Biñang". None of these items is the Biñang in Bocaue, Bulacan.

Chapters: **Pre-colonial**, **Spanish colonial**, **American period** (1898–1946), **WWII**, **Post-war/contemporary**.

## B. Leads that are promising but were NOT downloaded, or whose licence is unclear (to request)

1. **Robert L. Pendleton photographs, Manila South Road "Biñan–Muntinlupa", 6 Dec 1934** (American period). These are the only real pre-war photographs of the Biñan area found. American Geographical Society Library, UW–Milwaukee:
   - "Laguna province (Philippines), on Manila south road" – https://collections.lib.uwm.edu/digital/collection/agsphoto/id/14221
   - "…water buffaloes on the Manila south road" (note: "Looking toward Binan") – …/id/14222
   - "…four year old Brazilian fire tree" (same roadside) – …/id/14218
   - also …/id/14219, …/id/14220 (same roll, 409.x)
   - Rights: **In Copyright** (rightsstatements.org InC 1.0; rights holder Board of Regents, Univ. of Wisconsin System). The photographer died in 1957, so the copyright in the unpublished work runs to the end of 2027. **Ask AGSL for permission** (agsl@uwm.edu).
2. **University of Michigan "Philippine photographs" / Worcester collections** (quod.lib.umich.edu, collection `philpics` and related). Cloudflare blocked automated access, so these could not be searched. Search "Biñan"/"Binang" by hand; many items there are restricted to non-commercial use.
3. **NARA RG 18-AA "Airscapes": "Philippine Island – Luzon Island"**, about 1930s–1944 (file unit https://catalog.archives.gov/id/68144181, 223 items, public domain). These are aerial photos with typed captions ("AREA 18. PHILIPPINE ISLANDS – LUZON ISLAND – [PROV.] – [TOWN]"). The catalog titles are generic, and about 85 of the 168 Commons copies were checked without finding Biñan (Los Baños is there). The rest, including any LAGUNA PROV. captions, still need a visual check; there may be a Biñan or lakeshore view.
4. **Biblioteca Virtual de Defensa (Spain)**, CC BY 4.0. The viewer is JavaScript-only, so these could not be downloaded automatically. Search "Laguna de Bay" at https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es:
   - *Plano de la Bahía de Manila con todos sus contornos incluso la Laguna de Bay* (1802), 1:205,000
   - *Luzón* general map 1:135,000 (1897?)
   - *Descripción de la laguna de Bay … desecación de las vegas inundadas* (Malaspina expedition papers)
5. **AMS Luzon 1:50,000 Sheet 3263 II** (adjoining sheet south/east of San Pedro: Santa Rosa–Calamba). It covers Biñan's southern barangays (San Francisco/Halang, Zapote, Malamig, Timbao) but is not online at PCL. Public domain; request from PCL, the Library of Congress G&M Division, or the AGSL map collection. Also look for the 1944–45 WWII AMS/AFPAC 1:25,000 and 1:50,000 Luzon sheets covering Biñan (Series S712/S713); PCL has only "Mount Santa Rosa" online.
6. **Pedro Murillo Velarde 1734, Gallica (BnF) copies** (16- and 5-part scans on Commons, "Carta hydrographica … btv1b532941706"). These are duplicates of A1 and are not needed unless a different impression is wanted.
7. **Francisco Coello, 1852, "Islas Filipinas" (Geographicus scan)**, public domain: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1852_Coello_-_Morata_Case_Map_of_the_Philippines_-_Geographicus.jpg. The scale is small; check whether Biñan is legible. The download was hit by the Commons rate limit.
8. ***Pictorial album on Rizal* (José Rizal National Centennial Commission, 1962)**, Commons PDF https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pictorial_album_on_Rizal_(JRNCC,_1962).pdf. It may contain photographs of Rizal's school/house site in Biñan. Philippine government works carry no copyright (RA 8293 s.176), but commercial use needs prior approval from the issuing agency (now the NHCP), and the individual photographs may have private origins. **Verify before use.**
9. **Encarnación Alzona portrait** (born Biñan 1895). The Commons file https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Encarnacion_Alzona_NAST.jpg is tagged public domain (NAST official portrait), but it is only 226×297 px, too small to print. Ask NAST for a high-resolution copy.
10. **Philippines military memorandum book and map** (von Brandis, 1901), https://archive.org/details/philippinesmilit00bran, public domain. The folding map was not in the scan; the Library of Congress original may have it.
11. **1903 Census of the Philippine Islands, vol. 1** (https://archive.org/details/ajb5834.0001.001.umich.edu), public domain. It names Biñan only as a weather-station row in temperature tables, with no map or photo of Biñan. Low priority.
12. **Library of Congress P&P**: no Biñan items. Laguna-province context only: "Provincial government building, Laguna" (https://www.loc.gov/item/00651233/); "Pasig River boats carrying U.S. soldiers to Laguna de Bay", 1899 (https://www.loc.gov/item/2002709583/), useful for the 1899 Laguna campaign. Check the rights field of each item before use.

### Sources searched with no Biñan results
Wikimedia Commons (all suggested categories and searches: every Biñan photograph there dates from 2010 or later); Library of Congress search, maps and photos APIs; Internet Archive advancedsearch; NARA catalog (Biñan, Binan, Binang, Laguna, Manila Railroad Co. album RG 350-MR, 1,236 images, with no Biñan station photo found); UWM AGSL maps. Overpass and OSM were not used, as instructed.
